# Project Vision

## Multi-Sport Tournament Management Platform

### Executive Summary
This document outlines the vision for a Multi-Sport Tournament Management Platform designed for colleges. The platform enables sports administrators to create, manage, and run tournaments across multiple sports (starting with Cricket, Football, and Volleyball) while providing real-time score updates to students and spectators via a web interface.

### Product Vision
To become the go-to platform for college sports tournament management, streamlining operations for organizers and enhancing engagement for participants and spectators through real-time, accessible sports event data.

### Target Users
- **Admin**: System administrators managing platform settings, user roles, and overall system health.
- **Sports Coordinator**: College staff responsible for creating tournaments, registering teams, scheduling matches, and assigning scorers.
- **Scorer**: Designated individuals (often students or staff) who input match scores in real-time via sport-specific interfaces.
- **Audience/Students**: Students and spectators who view live scores, match schedules, and tournament brackets via public dashboards.

### Success Metrics
- **Organizer Efficiency**: Reduce tournament setup time by 70% compared to manual methods
- **Engagement**: Achieve 80%+ student viewer participation for live matches
- **Accuracy**: 0% manual scoring errors through validation and real-time updates
- **Scalability**: Support 500+ concurrent viewers without performance degradation
- **Adoption**: 90% of sports coordinators reporting reduced administrative overhead

### Assumptions & Dependencies
- Colleges have reliable internet access during events
- Users are comfortable with web-based interfaces
- Sport-specific scoring rules are well-defined and stable
- Platform will be deployed in a cloud environment (AWS/Azure/GCP)
- Initial MVP focuses on three sports with extension points for others
